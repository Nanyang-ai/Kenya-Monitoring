Kenya — Civil Unrest & Operational Risk Monitoring
Updated: 26 June 2026
Key Insights
Current Situation
  • 25 June protests proceeded across Nairobi — CBD sealed off, tear gas deployed, 355 arrested nationwide; no confirmed fatalities, a significant improvement on 2024 and 2025 anniversaries.
  • Heavy pre-emptive security operation effectively contained demonstrations — Nairobi CBD deserted by 9am; protesters constrained to University Way, did not reach Parliament.
  • Ebola facility remains an active secondary flashpoint — Health CS in contempt of court, Doctors Union strike ultimatum outstanding.
Key Changes
  • No fatalities confirmed on 25 June — security operation prevented the violence seen in 2024 (60 killed) and 2025 (16 killed).
  • 355 arrested nationwide; Interior Minister Murkomen apologised for the inconvenience of barricades — a notably conciliatory tone versus previous years.
  • CBD effectively shut for the day — roadblocks on all major arterials, matatus barred from entering, businesses closed.
Key Implications
  • CBD operations can resume today — situation has stabilised; no sustained unrest or property damage confirmed.
  • Saba Saba (7 July) is the next mobilisation date — monitor for further protest calls as the government's security-first approach has not resolved underlying grievances.
  • Anger remains directed at government policy, not Western businesses — no change to targeting risk.
